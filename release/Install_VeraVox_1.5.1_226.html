VeraVox 1.5.1

VeraVox 1.5.0 was one of our richest updates, and the feedback you sent after installing it helped us spot a few small things we wanted to put right without waiting. This update takes care of them, and rounds off some of the features 1.5.0 introduced. Your library, queues, saved playlists and settings are untouched.

The Signal Path

  • Since 1.5.0 the Signal Path also shows what happens to a file stored in floating point, such as a 32-bit float WAV, AIFF or WavPack, played in exclusive mode. Your DAC receives integer samples, so VeraVox delivers the music as integers at the highest resolution your DAC offers, without ever touching your file. The Signal Path says so, with Faithful Decode and a Float → Integer step instead of Bit-Perfect: not a compromise, but VeraVox telling you exactly what reaches your DAC, as it always does. The Stream Inspector and the Web Remote say the same.
  • This update corrects how ALAC files are shown. They are integer files that reach your DAC bit-perfect, and the Signal Path now shows them as Bit-Perfect, keeping Faithful Decode for files that really are in floating point. Their sound was never affected.

The Library

  • Inside an artist, a genre, a composer, a Smart Playlist or a search, an album counts the tracks that belong there, opens on them and plays them. When they are only part of the album it says so, as in "3 of 12 tracks", and Whole Album in its header shows the rest. The Web Remote does the same.
  • In the Columns layout of the Artists tab, select several albums, or All Albums, and the track list below follows.
  • Export the Dynamic Range reports of several albums in one go, with Export Dynamic Range Reports… in the right-click menu of a selection. Each report goes into its album's folder, or all into a folder you choose, and tracks not yet analysed can be measured in the same pass. The work runs in the background, from a NAS too, and a new export picks up where the last one stopped.

Other fixes

  • On macOS 27, the Mini Player and the diagnostic panels move again when you drag them from anywhere that isn't a control.
  • Smart Playlists sorted by Play Count or Last Played, Play Count rules and the sort direction all work as they should.
  • Single-file CUE albums in ALAC reach network renderers at full resolution.
  • Tuneshine displays that are slow to respond get their cover more reliably.
