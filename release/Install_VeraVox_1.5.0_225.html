VeraVox 1.5.0

A queue you can take back, multiple selection and a Columns view in the Library, a Dynamic Range report to export, and a good number of fixes. Your library, queues, saved playlists and settings are untouched, except for the Dynamic Range figures: see below.

The queue

  • Playing from the Library still replaces the queue, but the old one is no longer lost: a notice offers Restore for a few seconds, and Restore Previous Queue in the Queue's ⋯ menu brings it back for the rest of the session, on the Web Remote too.
  • Option-click a track's play icon, or choose Play Now, Keep Queue, to play it without replacing the queue. New Playlist… in the right-click menu of a track, an album or an artist makes a saved playlist directly.
  • With Shuffle on, a queue starts from a random track, or from the one you pick, and the shuffled order follows the queue as you change it. At the end of the queue, Play starts it again.

The Library

  • Select several tracks or albums with click, ⌘-click, ⇧-click and ⌘A, then right-click to play, queue, add to a playlist, favourite, rate or analyse them all at once. Esc steps back one level at a time.
  • On the Artists tab, a large enough window offers a Columns layout: artists and albums side by side, tracks below. An artist can also show all of their tracks in one sortable list.
  • Back and Forward in the Library: from the Library menu (⌘[ and ⌘]), with ⌘← and ⌘→, with the mouse's side buttons, or with a two-finger swipe when Swipe between pages is set that way in System Settings.
  • Track lists are tidier: durations never wrap, DR and LRA line up in columns, a narrow pane leaves more room to the title, and every row's right-click menu sets a favourite or a rating.
  • Accented and non-Latin titles read correctly in more files, and Smart Playlist text rules ignore accents and case. Rebuild This Folder… in the Folder Manager refreshes files already in the library.

Dynamic Range

  • The DR value follows the published measurement procedure in every detail and matches the reference meters and the community DR database track for track. To keep every figure consistent, values computed by earlier versions are cleared once, at the first launch. To measure again, right-click an album and choose Compute Dynamic Range: one album at a time, whenever you like. Tools ▸ Analyze Library Dynamic Range… measures the whole library in one pass, which takes a long time on a large library, and longer from a network share or a NAS.
  • The DR shown on an album is the official album value, the rounded average of its tracks, with the minimum and maximum in the tooltip.
  • Export an album's Dynamic Range report as a PDF or a text log, from the album's right-click menu or from Track Info. It is proposed in the album's own folder, where the Booklet opens it. The analysis also measures RMS.

Other fixes

  • Gapless playback between tracks of different formats, in single-file CUE albums and in a queue restored at launch; a change to the queue midway through a track now reaches the track that follows.
  • Single-file CUE albums show each track's own duration and measurements.
  • Playback from a network share or a NAS starts faster and is steadier on the first listen.
  • Some mono and floating-point files, and some multichannel files on shared outputs, now play as they should.
  • Stop, Play and Pause respond the same way from every control, Control Center and headphones included.
  • More reliable track changes with network renderers and in UPnP Renderer Mode.
  • Smaller fixes to Choose Cover with many images, to album headers opened from the player, and to the Web Remote's menus and scrolling on iPhone and iPad.

Most of the improvements above began with someone taking the time to write in and describe what they saw.
